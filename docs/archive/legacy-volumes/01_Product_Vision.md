# FOLIO

## Volume 1 — Product Vision & Philosophy

### Version 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

## What is Folio?

Folio is a premium digital notebook designed to recreate the satisfaction of writing in a beautiful physical notebook while providing the convenience of modern digital tools.

Folio is **not** intended to compete with OneNote, Notion, Obsidian, or Google Docs as an all-purpose productivity platform. Instead, it focuses on creating a calm, distraction-free environment where writing, thinking, sketching, and organizing ideas feel natural.

The software should make users want to open it simply because the experience of writing is enjoyable.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

> Build the most enjoyable digital notebook for Windows—one that combines the elegance of premium stationery with the convenience of modern software.
> 

The application should feel handcrafted, refined, and intuitive. Every interaction should reinforce the feeling that the notebook is the primary object, while the software quietly supports the user in the background.

---

# 3. Mission

Create a notebook that users genuinely enjoy opening every day.

Every animation, interaction, and visual element should make the experience smoother, calmer, and more delightful than writing on plain digital documents.

---

# 4. Core Philosophy

Every decision should follow these principles:

### Writing First

Writing is the main purpose of the application.

Everything else should support writing, not interrupt it.

---

### Simplicity

The interface should never overwhelm users.

Advanced features should remain available but should not clutter the workspace.

---

### Premium Feel

The application should feel carefully crafted.

High-quality animations, spacing, typography, and transitions matter as much as functionality.

---

### Realistic, Not Literal

The goal is **not** to copy a paper notebook exactly.

Instead, improve upon it.

Examples:

Physical notebook

↓

Digital equivalent

Pages can be searched instantly.

Bookmarks never fall out.

Undo is always available.

Infinite pen colors.

Instant exports.

Zoom.

No ink smudges.

---

### Fast

The application should feel immediate.

Users should never notice delays while writing or navigating.

# 9. Product Principles

Every new feature should satisfy at least one of these:

Makes writing easier

Makes reading easier

Makes organization easier

Makes the notebook more beautiful

Makes the software feel faster

If it does none of these, it probably doesn't belong.

A notebook.

A sketchbook.

A research notebook.

# 11. What Folio Is Not

---

# 14. User Interface Philosophy

The notebook should always be the largest and most important element on the screen.

Toolbars should stay out of the way until needed.

Panels should float rather than dominate.

The application should feel like a workspace, not a dashboard.

---

# 15. Animation Philosophy

Animations are not decoration.

They communicate state changes and make interactions feel natural.

Animations should be:

Smooth

Fast

Predictable

Purposeful

Avoid excessive or distracting effects.

---

# 16. Performance Philosophy

Writing should feel instantaneous.

The application should remain responsive even with:

Large notebooks

Many pages

Numerous images

Complex drawings

Smooth performance is a feature, not an optimization.

---

# 17. Quality Standards

Every release should meet these standards:

No obvious bugs.

No broken layouts.

No lag during writing.

Consistent spacing.

Consistent animations.

Responsive controls.

Polished visuals.

If a feature is incomplete, it should be hidden until ready.

---

# 18. Success Criteria

Folio is successful when users say:

- "This feels better than writing in my physical notebook."
- "I actually enjoy taking notes here."
- "The software disappears, and I can focus on my ideas."
- "It feels premium without being complicated."
